Taking a cue from Melville's Bartleby, the Scrivener, who answers not 'I will not' but 'I would prefer not to,' I look to the history of refusal for responses that protest the terms of the question itself. That creative space of refusal is threatened in a time of widespread economic precarity, when everyone from Amazon workers to college students see their margin of refusal shrinking, and the stakes for playing along growing.
