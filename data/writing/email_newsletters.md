Volume 1\| November 24th, 2018
**Welcome to Volume \#1 of the new Modest Operations email newsletter!**

After 10\+ years in the 9\-5 corporate world, Modest Operations is my new Operations\-focused consulting business. Even if that sounds incredibly dry or irrelevant to you, hopefully you'll get something useful out of these emails. 

This email newsletter is a bit of an experiment, but I hope you'll follow along with me. The basic gist is that I'll be sending a list of my favorite things across the web on a (somewhat) weekly basis. I've been doing something similar with my [personal blog](https://modestoperations.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a27f2afdab18287b408e923fc&id=eace3be8c1&e=7098826cb3) for the last few years, and am taking it to a new medium (and new name).

I've personally been a fan of email newsletters as a medium for awhile, and I'd be remiss if I didn't mention a few of the great creators, designers, writers, friends, and colleagues I've drawn on for inspiration. Some of my favorites include \- [Just Another Crowd](https://modestoperations.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a27f2afdab18287b408e923fc&id=f42f778b61&e=7098826cb3), [Dense Discovery](https://modestoperations.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a27f2afdab18287b408e923fc&id=d67daac3fc&e=7098826cb3), [Hurry Slowly](https://modestoperations.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a27f2afdab18287b408e923fc&id=38bfb4815c&e=7098826cb3), [Startup Resources](https://modestoperations.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a27f2afdab18287b408e923fc&id=97b0f777fb&e=7098826cb3), [Royal Braun](https://modestoperations.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a27f2afdab18287b408e923fc&id=88d12f732b&e=7098826cb3), [For The Interested](https://modestoperations.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a27f2afdab18287b408e923fc&id=b8e450dd56&e=7098826cb3), [Stay Hipp](https://modestoperations.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a27f2afdab18287b408e923fc&id=a13a70490a&e=7098826cb3), and [READ.LOOK.THINK](https://modestoperations.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a27f2afdab18287b408e923fc&id=081a22b8c1&e=7098826cb3).
