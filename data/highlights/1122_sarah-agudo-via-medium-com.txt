I’m into commencement addresses lately. They’re a weird genre at which dark, tortured souls seem to unexpectedly excel. Maybe there’s something about asking them to be light but heady; to indulge in scaring grads without immobilizing them with the futility of it all; to elegantly handle the fact that a graduation speech is both an honor and awkwardly off-brand for the type of person worth having.
