How do you spot them? Anybody who talks big in meetings with big statements but doesn’t follow up. On the other hand, there are some that are quiet and “thinking” throughout and say “let’s think on that” because they have no idea what’s going on. The worst are thought-leaders. I always think what a great job they have, “leading thought.” Wow. What does their Monday morning look like? They think so big they can never be accountable. I think every company needs to have like a chief bullshit officer to sniff those people out.
