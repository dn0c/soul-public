My whole life I’ve felt like a tourist. Like when you step off the bus in a foreign country and everyone is speaking a language you don’t understand, engaged in what seems like a practiced and coordinated ballet that doesn’t involve you. Except that’s what it’s like for me in most everyday situations. The perpetual sense that I missed the memo.
