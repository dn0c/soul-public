# Data Directory

This folder contains raw source material—your actual content that the LLM can reference for grounding.

### twitter_archive.md — Twitter/X Archive

- `twitter_archive.md` — Raw tweet data (2019–2020)

Used for: understanding posting rhythm, tone on social media, quick-reaction voice, how you respond to things in the moment.

### this_week.md — Curated Reading Digest (2013–2018)

- `this_week.md` — "This Week" link posts only (2013–2018): curated quotes and links from articles Drew read and found worth sharing

Used for: highest-signal curation data. These posts are pure editorial judgment — what you chose to surface and why. More signal-dense than the full blog archive for understanding intellectual taste.

### writing/ — Long-form Content

Your articles, blog posts, essays, newsletters. Mix of Markdown files and Google Docs (.gdoc).

- `linkedin.md` — LinkedIn bio and posts, including the 40th birthday career retrospective
- `personal_notes.md` — Raw journal entries (Mar–Jun 2026); most recent thinking on career, C3P, Personal LLM SEO, Domestic Cozy
- `blog_posts.md` — Full blog archive (2010–2018): "This Week" link posts + "Other Posts" (essays, opinions, photo posts). Superset of this_week.md with more personal/editorial content.
- `email_newsletters.md` — Newsletter content
- `blog.drewshannon.net - Tumblr Text Archive` — Full Tumblr archive
- Various .gdoc files (Turning 40, StayHipp post, etc.)

Used for: grounding on positions, understanding long-form voice, referencing when asked about topics you've written on. `personal_notes.md` is the most current and emotionally honest signal.

### influences.md — Intellectual Influences

Who and what shaped your thinking. More detailed than the Influences section in SOUL.md.

Includes: people, books, concepts, frameworks, and what you took from each.

### peer_feedback/ — External Validation

Structured assessments and feedback from others. High-signal for understanding how you're perceived vs. how you see yourself.

- `disc_assessment.md` — DISC profile (Precisionist CS: systematic, detail-oriented, facts-driven)
- `johnson_oconnor.md` — Aptitude testing results (Analytical Reasoning, Objective Personality)
- `peer_bonuses.md` — 40+ peer bonuses from colleagues (2019–2026); rich qualitative signal on how teammates describe your impact

Used for: grounding identity claims with external evidence, understanding the gap (or alignment) between self-perception and how others see you.

### Coaching Notes — Career & Identity Work

Notes from ongoing coaching relationships. Some of the most emotionally honest material in this folder.

- `kaylin_coaching_notes.md` — Sessions with coach Kaylin (2023). Focus: work-identity entanglement, boundary-setting, the "should" saboteur, scaffolding, the "Happy Drew" ideal day.
- `bryan_collins_coach_meeting_notes.md` — Sessions with executive coach Bryan Collins / Poetic State (2026). Focus: career anxiety, the "brick by brick" framework, C3P strategy, next chapter exploration.

Used for: understanding the emotional undercurrent beneath the professional narrative, career direction grounding, identity tensions that don't show up in formal writing.

### highlights/ — Curation Archive

830+ saved articles (Readwise/Instapaper export). Represents years of reading history and intellectual curation across topics: productivity, design, music, media, food, career, tech, culture.

Used for: understanding intellectual range and taste, identifying recurring themes and obsessions, calibrating what Drew finds worth saving vs. skimming. Browse by filename — titles are descriptive. Don't inject wholesale; use for pattern recognition.

## Tips

- **More is better** (up to a point): The LLM can browse this to understand you better.
- **Quality matters**: Curate for content that represents your best/most authentic voice.
- **Organize sensibly**: Use folders and clear filenames.
- **Update regularly**: Add new content as you produce it.

## How It's Used

The LLM uses data/ for:
1. **Grounding**: When asked about a topic you've written on
2. **Tone calibration**: Understanding how you write in different contexts
3. **Position reference**: Checking your stated views on specific things
4. **Pattern extraction**: The soul-builder uses this to draft your soul files

The LLM browses data/ but doesn't inject it wholesale. It absorbs the vibe and references specifics when relevant.
