The genius of node-based interfaces is that they externalize your mental model. Beyond convenient UI sugar, they're a structural shift in how we represent computation. The canvas leverages what cognitive scientists call "external scaffolding"—offloading mental complexity into the physical (or in this case, visual) world. Instead of holding a 50-line Python script in your memory, you glance at the canvas and see the flow. Your visual cortex's extraordinary pattern-recognition abilities do the heavy lifting.
