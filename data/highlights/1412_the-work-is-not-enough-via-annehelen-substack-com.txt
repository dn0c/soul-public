For a long time, this is how I understood my own burnout: I was depleted and overextended because of the work I did. The solution then, must be to figure out how to not do as much work, or different kinds of work. But that's always felt both unsatisfying and insufficient. The actual problem is that I, like a lot of people, have organized my sense of self entirely around work. And when work started to feel bad, or irrelevant, or insufficient — when it could no longer do the very particular things I was asking it to do — I was adrift.
