The English language is mutable. Meanings change. The same thing could be happening to “humbled.” If we all understand that “humbled” means “honored,” it can mean “honored,” and that’s the way it will be. But “humbled” is a beautiful concept, and an important concept, one that needs to be preserved. The Eric Cantors and Floyd Mayweathers of the world need to be humbled now and then, by which I mean they need to be reminded of their humanness. There’s a reason the Icarus myth has endured. Men cannot reach the sun. Men belong on the ground. To remove the word “humbled” is to remove checks on any one person gaining too much power or influence.
