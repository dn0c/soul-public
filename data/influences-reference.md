# Influences

Narrative reference for why each person matters to Drew — distinct from the roleplay personas in `influences.md`.

*   **Brian Eno**: For lateral thinking, creative systems, and using constraints as inspiration ("honor thy error as a hidden intention").
*   **David Heinemeier Hansson (DHH)**: For radical efficiency, lean systems, and protecting time. "Reconsider" is a foundational text.
*   **Stewart Brand**: For long-term systems thinking and the discipline of maintenance. "Stay hungry, stay foolish."
*   **Larry Page**: For pushing to think 10x bigger and leveraging scale.
*   **Marshall McLuhan**: For the understanding that "the medium is the message" (critical for platform strategy at YouTube).
*   **Jon Gruber**: For meticulous focus on UI/UX, tech craftsmanship, and independent writing. The gold standard for principled independent technical writing.
*   **Daiki Suzuki**: For the curation of functional heritage — work that is durable, practical, and aesthetically timeless. Guides how to look at the "construction" of ideas.
*   **Anthony Bourdain**: For raw truth, cultural empathy, and uncompromising honesty. "Show up, be humble, and eat what's put in front of you." An aesthete with a bone-deep love of order and belonging.
*   **Ian Rogers**: A professional North Star — bridging digital music disruption (Winamp, Yahoo Music) with high-end aesthetics (Apple, LVMH). Where interests in gear and brands meet platform strategy.
*   **Jack Conte**: As CEO of Patreon, a fellow musician and gear-head who built systems to solve the "starving artist" problem.
*   **Yancey Strickler**: Co-founder of Kickstarter, now building infrastructure for the new private internet (Artist Corporations, the Dark Forest Collective). A tech entrepreneur with similar beliefs in using the power of technology to bring people together in more humane ways and help them rise to their potential.
*   **Austin Kleon**: For the ethics of making work public — creative practice as generosity, incremental and unglamorous. *Show Your Work* is less a book about self-promotion and more an operational argument for why documentation is part of the work itself. The overlap with PKM and "if it doesn't have a URL, it probably didn't happen" is not accidental.
*   **Frank Chimero**: For writing about design and the web with substance and self-awareness. *The Shape of Design* is the design equivalent of building a second brain — a thoughtful framework for what it means to make something with intention. Chimero's blog writing models the kind of considered, slow-moving creative thinking Drew aspires to.
*   **Bryan Collins**: Executive coach (Poetic State). Helped articulate the "brick by brick" body-of-work framework and reduce career anxiety. Asks the breakthrough questions.
*   **Ted Chiang**: Science fiction writer whose work explores AI, consciousness, language, and time while maintaining deep human warmth. "His fiction may take flights of fancy, but it has a human heart." Found via MR PORTER (not Wired) — the fashion-to-fiction crossover is telling. Speaks to the art-technology intersection Drew lives in.
*   **Chris Walla** (Death Cab for Cutie): "Darkness may find me, but I shall never choose it" — a moral compass statement worth keeping. Persistent presence in the Are.na archive.
*   **Bill Hoglund (Grandfather)**: Former GM Pontiac & Saturn chief; for a legacy of leadership defined by integrity, decency, and fierce advocacy for the customer.
