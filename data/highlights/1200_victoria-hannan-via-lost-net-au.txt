No one I knew had been to California. My Dad was the only person who could attest to the Hollywood sign really being there. He was the only person who could confirm that what I’d seen on TV, in films, in photos in books was actually something that could be seen. That the world was really out there, really a living breathing functioning magical thing that could be seen and had and felt.
