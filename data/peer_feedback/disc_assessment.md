# Drew Shannon - DISC Assessment

**Communication Style:** Precisionist CS

## Personal Style Description

  * **Systematic Thinker:** Drew is a systematic thinker who follows orderly, predetermined procedures in both personal and professional life.
  * **Attention to Detail:** He is precise, attentive to detail, and requires accuracy in his work while maintaining high standards.
  * **Diplomatic and Tactful:** Drew acts in a highly diplomatic fashion and rarely consciously antagonizes associates.
  * **Social Preferences:** He enjoys people but prefers small groups and a few close friends over large crowds.
  * **Decision Making:** Drew prefers having all exact facts and figures before making a decision and may feel uneasy when forced to make quick choices.
  * **Environment:** He thrives in a stable, organized, and non-confrontational environment that promotes security.
  * **Conflict Avoidance:** He tends to avoid conflict and sudden changes, often keeping feelings to himself rather than blowing up under pressure.

## Summary Characteristics

  * **General Characteristics:** Careful, methodical, sensitive, practical, loyal, predictable, disciplined, and logical.
  * **Motivations:** Drew is motivated by recognition for loyalty, approval of high-quality work, orderliness, and seeing activities through to the end.
  * **Ideal Environment:** An environment with practical procedures, stability, predictability, neatness, and a team atmosphere.
