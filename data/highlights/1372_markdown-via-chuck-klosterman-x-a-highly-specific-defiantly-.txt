It's not uncommon for popular people to claim they were once unpopular, so I ask Swift for a specific example. She tells a story about junior high, when she called several of her peers on the phone and asked if they wanted to go shopping. Every girl had a different excuse for why she couldn't go. Eventually, Swift's mother agreed to take her to the local mall. When they arrived, Swift saw all of the girls she had called on the phone, goofing around in Victoria's Secret. "I just remember my mom looking at me when this happened and saying, 'We're going to King of Prussia Mall, right now.' Which is the biggest mall in Pennsylvania, forty-five minutes away. So we left and went to the better mall. My mom let me escape from certain things that were too painful to deal with. And we talked about it the whole way there, and we had a good time shopping."

Source: via Chuck Klosterman X: A Highly Specific, Defiantly Incomplete History of the Early 21st Century by Chuck Klosterman
