I couldn’t leave the bottle alone, and the phone’s the same way these days, so I’m asking myself what I’m getting out of it.  Affirmation: Did anyone like anything I posted, wrote or texted?  Shopping: When’s that new journal, record, book or bag going to arrive? Will checking give me a small echo of the feeling when I bought it?  Notetaking: I should remind myself to buy candles and the unwaxed floss my hygienist recommended.  Scrolling: Reminding myself how much I hate Reddit somehow requires me to scroll through reference jokes and weird screeds for 45 minutes at a time.  Socializing: I should heart some posts now and then.
