When he sings, 'Those rusty pipes do nothing but complain,' he's not only referring to the pipes. The people that he draws into these soggy lives are too tired to put up too much of a fight. They aren't going to roll over, but they're grizzled enough to know that it's better to just silently sop up the flooded basement, rip out the carpet if you have to, replace that tire with a scribble on a check and just ready one's self for the next time it will inevitably happen.
