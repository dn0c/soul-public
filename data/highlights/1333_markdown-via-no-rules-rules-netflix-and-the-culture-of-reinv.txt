Any leader who tries to be more transparent quickly recognizes that the good of bringing things out in the open sometimes competes with the good of respecting an individual's right to privacy. Both are important. But when someone is let go, everyone wants to understand why. What happened will eventually come out. But if you explain plainly and honestly why you've fired someone, gossip ceases and trust increases.

Source: via No Rules Rules: Netflix and the Culture of Reinvention by Reed Hastings and Erin Meyer
