"Facebook has a heavy meeting cultureat least I know it does on my teamso I try to protect my calendar as much as I can. I will put in blocks of time so that people cant block me out. If something is important theyll reach out via email or messenger and Ill open up a time. I found that helps me stay focused on the things I think I should be focused on. I have a lot of team meetings and product meetings, as is, so protecting my calendar has been a very good exercise and productivity boost for me."

Geoff Teehan, Product Design Director at Facebook
